About Garrison
Local, Bilingual,
On Your Side

Garrison Housing Solutions was started by Tenny Vongtip, a US Army veteran who lives in Poznań. He knows what it's like to arrive in Poland, open a rental listing and not understand a word of it.

Most agencies here work for the landlord. We work for you, the tenant. Our job is to find you a flat that suits you, make sure the terms are fair, and make sure you understand everything you sign.

We handle conversations with Polish landlords and property managers in Polish and explain everything to you in clear English. When something breaks or a letter arrives that you can't read, you'll have someone to call.

The military background shows in how we work: we answer quickly, keep you updated, and tell you plainly what's going on.
